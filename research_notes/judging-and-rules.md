# Judging, rules and last year's winners

Researched 2026-11-01. The official judging guide and terms pages could not be fetched from the research machine (local TLS certificate error, deliberately not bypassed), so most of this is from secondary sources. Re-check the official pages.

## How judging works

- Local or Universal Event judges choose the Global Nominees.
- NASA and Space Agency Partner subject-matter experts review nominees to choose Global Finalists and Honorable Mentions.
- An executive committee of agency leaders and executives picks the 10 Global Winners.
- Judges come from NASA, academia and industry. No individual judges are named in public sources.
- 2026 hackathon: Nov 14-15. Judging begins Nov 16.

## What is rewarded (secondary sources, verify)

- A 2024 guide summary lists five criteria: Influence, Creativity, Validity, Relevance, Presentation. Older rounds used impact, creativity, product, sustainability, presentation.
- Presenting a high-level summary and a demo is itself a judged criterion.
- A coaching site claims points are lost for not using the provided NASA datasets (unofficial).
- Reading: scientific validity is a gate for the expert round; a story an agency leader can repeat matters in the executive round.

## Rules

- AI tools are permitted, but teams must clearly indicate where and how AI was used (from an older GitHub discussion; confirm in the current terms).
- Original content must be open source; Apache 2.0 applies by default.
