About Sol Lab

A health tool for astronauts. Each crew member logs a few measurements a day; the tool compares them with that person's own normal, says plainly whether anything needs attention and what to do, and helps test which changes actually help.

Why it matters

Far from Earth, crews must judge their own health. Comparing each person with their own normal catches changes that population ranges miss, and a fair self-test stops anyone trusting a lucky streak. The same approach helps people far from a doctor on Earth: polar and offshore crews, rural patients, anyone tracking a long-term condition.

Using it

Tabs along the top open each station; click objects in the module to jump to them. Space plays the mission, 1 to 0 switch tabs, Left and Right move between stations, Shift with Left or Right steps a day. The 1 h/s speed runs the daily routine with reminders. Each tab has an "About this view" section at the bottom with its sources.

Real data

  • ISS radiation: 123,695 DOSTEL-1 readings (DLR), plus six-module REM data, NASA OSDR RadLab.
  • ISS cabin air: 358 days of CO2, temperature and humidity, NASA OSDR Environmental Data App.
  • Bloodwork: the four Inspiration4 astronauts (SpaceX, 2021), NASA OSDR OSD-569 and OSD-575.
  • Sleep: diaries of 49 people, Baigutanova et al. 2025 (CC BY).
  • Food: USDA FoodData Central. Orbit: CelesTrak elements with SGP4.
  • Anatomy: BodyParts3D © The Database Center for Life Science (CC BY 4.0), via open-anatomy-3d (github.com/MervinR07/open-anatomy-3d, CC BY 4.0); structures merged by body system and recoloured as a hologram by Sol Lab. Space weather: NASA DONKI. 3D models: NASA 3D Resources.

Heart rate, blood pressure, blood sugar, mood and exercise for days nobody logged are simulated and labelled as such. Records from different people and years are combined into one mission.

The five hazards

NASA's Human Research Program groups spaceflight risks into five hazards: radiation, isolation and confinement, distance from Earth, altered gravity, and a hostile closed environment. Each is tracked by at least one station.

Method

Guidance levels follow published cut-offs (ACC/AHA, ADA, CDC, AHA, AASM); where none exists the team chose one and marked it for clinician review. The self-experiment engine was checked in pre-registered studies on real data (false calls 0.68%). Details: docs on GitHub.
